What I did

  • Modeled the airframe in OpenVSP and showed the wing area was undersized for the mission
  • Built a hover endurance calculator with an ISA atmosphere, motor model, and battery discharge model, validated against eCalc
  • Ran configuration trades that ruled out VTOL conversion on mass budget, in favor of catapult launch and parachute recovery
  • Ranked propulsion layouts; a single nose-mounted tractor came out on top
  • Analyzed multirotor hover power at altitude for comparison
