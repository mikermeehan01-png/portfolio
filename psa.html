Cloud seeding support for Alto Cirra

PSA UAS does contract engineering for Alto Cirra, a Utah-based cloud seeding company. As the program changed direction, I've worked on each phase, from early airframe trades to the hardware now collecting data.

How the program evolved

  1. Phase 1

    VTOL concept

    Learned airframe design from the ground up, then ran propulsion estimates for several VTOL configurations, including a hover endurance calculator validated against eCalc.

    Ruled out: the VTOL hardware took too much of the mass budget.

  2. Phase 2

    Fixed-wing, rail launch and net recovery

    The program moved to a fixed-wing platform launched from a rail and recovered in a net. I modeled the airframe in OpenVSP.

    Shelved for a lower-cost way to start gathering data while Alto Cirra waits on funding.

  3. Phase 3

    10" drone testbeds

    Designed a GPS mount and a sensor enclosure for 10" drone testbeds, whose job is collecting atmospheric data to better understand the conditions for seeding.

  4. Phase 4

    Balloon-launched gliders

    Helping slice and 3D print a glider platform that launches from a weather balloon and carries sensors to collect data on the way down.
